# Business Model

Live list prices for RegASK/Enhesa/欧税通 SKU engines are **Not publicly disclosed**. Design below is a hypothesis, not a rate card we have charged.

## Free

1 SKU × 1 market. Acquisition. Watermark: not legal advice.

## Pro

100 SKU × 5 markets × change monitoring. Target: Guangdong electronics exporter. Price band to research with design partners (do not publish a fake CNY number).

## Enterprise

10k SKU × many markets × API × PIM/ERP. Security review, SSO, audit log.

## API

Bill SKU-checks, country packs, monitoring events.

## Expert marketplace

When the engine says “need CE-RED / BPOM / EU-RP”, refer labs and consultants. Referral or take-rate. This is also the liability buffer.

## Why someone pays

Avoiding one Amazon suppression or one detained container exceeds a Pro subscription. Buyer is the owner; user is 外贸/认证.

## Independence

Do not make Alibaba the only channel. CSV in; Alibaba/Amazon/TikTok/Temu/SHEIN/Shopify/ERP later.
