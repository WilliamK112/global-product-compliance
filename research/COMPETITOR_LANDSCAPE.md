# Competitor Landscape

Last verified: **2026-08-16**. Sources are official product/help pages unless marked otherwise. Pricing that is not on a public rate card is **Not publicly disclosed**.

This document is the long form. README only lists the 7 names judges need. Evaluation axis:

`Product × Country × Platform × Regulation → SKU impact → Action`

CanSell does **not** claim to have invented regulatory AI, SKU mapping, or change monitoring. The honest claim is a **novel combination**: Chinese-exporter catalog UX + evidence-bound market-access states + Alibaba.com and Amazon overlays + remediation re-check, on a **credible encoded subset**.

## Headline

**Already solved:** paying enterprises can buy RegASK/Enhesa; Amazon sellers can operate Manage Your Compliance.

**Open problem:** a factory selling the same catalog on Alibaba.com **and** Amazon still cannot see, with official evidence, which SKUs a destination-law or platform-rule change hits — then re-check after they attach certificates.

That combination is the only uniqueness claim.
