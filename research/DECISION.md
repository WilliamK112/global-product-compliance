# Project Decision Gate

**Decision: GO** (2026-08-16)

## Why not NO-GO

- Data for a **credible subset** is publicly obtainable (EUR-Lex, eCFR, FDA, BPOM Notifkos, DJID portal, Amazon PDFs, Alibaba seller blog).
- The job is **not fully solved** for Chinese SME catalogs across platforms. RegASK/Enhesa/Amazon are real; they leave this ICP.
- Liability is manageable if we never emit a silent PASS and never call output a legal opinion.
- Input works without Alibaba OpenAPI (CSV).
- Buyer exists (factory owner) even without a signed contract today.

## Why not PIVOT

- Pivoting to “law search” would fail GOAI (chatbot/search) and fail the thesis.
- Pivoting to medical devices would inherit Akang but explode liability and miss Boundless Agents demo clarity.
- Pivoting to tax/VAT would collide with 欧税通.

## Conditions of GO

- Primary vertical: consumer electronics.
- Geography: CN → EU + US + ID.
- Honest coverage language only.
- Separate repo from Akang and Pruva.
