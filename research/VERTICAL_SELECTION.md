## Decision

**ONE primary vertical: Consumer electronics** (wireless + mains lighting as two SKUs in the same industrial cluster).

**Bounded contrast SKU (not a second vertical commitment):** one cosmetic serum, because Indonesia BPOM vs US MoCRA vs EU 1223 is the clearest ASEAN BLOCKED cell, and Akang taught us not to fake BPOM. Cosmetics stays **demo-only** until electronics matching is solid.

**Not selected:** medical devices (liability + GOAI safety), food, apparel (too weak), standalone batteries (nested in electronics anyway).

## Country set

**China origin → EU + US + Indonesia.**

- EU: GPSR + RED/LVD/EMC public.
- US: FCC Part 15 + CPSC + FDA cosmetics guidance public.
- Indonesia: Notifkos + DJID certification portals public; SNI lighting marked WARNING-quality because mandatory lists move.

If coverage must shrink further: drop US only after Amazon overlay is no longer needed for the story. For this contest, keep US.

Alibaba.com + Amazon. Others documented, not encoded.
