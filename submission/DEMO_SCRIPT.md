# Demo script (90–120s)

Safety line first: **Not legal advice.**

1. Open the matrix. Four Guangdong SKUs: speaker, TWS earbuds, LED lamp, serum. EU / US / ID.
2. Point to **serum × Indonesia = BLOCKED**. Click. Show BPOM Notifkos URL, missing NIE, actions (local entity, PIF, submit).
3. Point to **speaker × EU = BLOCKED**. Click. RED 2014/53/EU Articles 3/10/17. CE-EMC is not RED. Missing RF assessment.
4. Point to **LED × EU = WARNING**. LVD/EMC evidenced; GPSR EU responsible person still missing. This is why we do not use yes/no.
5. Upload is CSV. Click **改首行 SKU 名再评估** — status stays the same. Attributes, not names.
6. Restore demo catalog. Click **Simulate regulation change**. Indonesian luminaire SNI version event. **1 of 4 SKUs affected (LED).** Before PASS (ID) → After WARNING. Speaker, earbuds, and serum unchanged.
7. Close: exporters do not need more news. They need affected SKUs and next actions.

If the API is down, run `pytest` and `uvicorn` as fallback evidence of a real engine.
