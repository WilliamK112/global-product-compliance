# Submission description (EN)

**Name:** CanSell — Global Product Compliance Agent  
**Track:** Boundless Agents · AI + Industrial Manufacturing  
**One-liner:** Know where every product can sell — before regulations stop it.

Chinese exporters are not short of regulatory news. They cannot see **which SKUs** a rule hits. CanSell is a product-aware market-access engine, not a law search box. It computes Product × Country × Platform × Regulation into PASS / WARNING / BLOCKED / UNCERTAIN / EXPERT_REVIEW_REQUIRED, each bound to an official URL, article, retrieval date, and hash. An LLM cannot assert alone.

MVP is a credible subset: consumer electronics (Bluetooth speaker, LED lamp) plus one cosmetics contrast SKU; EU, US, Indonesia; Alibaba.com and Amazon. A monitored regulation change re-evaluates the catalog and reports **which SKUs moved**.

Not legal advice. MIT. Runnable API, matrix UI, pytest, and documentation are in the repository.
